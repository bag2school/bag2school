Delete your account

You can delete your Bag2School account and its data at any time.

Home Privacy Delete account

Option 1 — inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open Bag2School and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Account.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.

Option 2 — by email

If you no longer have the app installed, send a request from the email address you used to sign up. We will verify the request and delete the account within 7 days.

Email: bag2school.project@gmail.com

Subject: Delete my Bag2School account

Please include the email address of the account. Do not send your password.

Send deletion request

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details (the email becomes free to use again).
  • Your profile and family membership.
  • Any family you created, including its children and invitations, unless ownership is transferred to a remaining member.

What stays on your phone

Schedules, preparation items and homework are stored on the device. They are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. They are not sent to us, so account deletion does not remove them remotely.
