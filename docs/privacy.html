Bag2School helps parents plan the school week: schedules, preparation checklists and homework, with a friendly mascot called Bagi. This page explains what we store, why, and how you can remove it.

What we collect

  • Account: email address, password (stored hashed by our authentication provider), and the display name you choose.
  • App content: family name, family member names, children's names, school grade and optional gender (so the avatar matches). You enter this data yourself.
  • Plan data: activities, preparation items and homework are stored on your device.
We do not collect photos of children.
Bag2School has no camera or photo library access, and child photos are never uploaded.

What we never do

  • We never sell your data.
  • We never show ads to children, and ads are never personalised for them.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs beyond the advertising described below.
  • No location, contacts, microphone or camera access.

Ads and subscriptions

Bag2School is free to use. The free version may show advertising, and an optional subscription removes those ads. When ads are shown:

  • Ads are provided by a third-party advertising partner, which may receive a device advertising identifier, coarse device information and the fact that an ad was shown or tapped.
  • We never pass your child's name, grade, family name or plan data to an advertising partner.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), you will be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change that choice later in the app or your device settings.
  • Subscribers see no ads, so no advertising identifier is used for them.

This page is updated with the name of the advertising partner before ads go live.

Where data lives

Account and family data are stored in our database provider (Supabase, hosted on managed cloud infrastructure) with row level security so one family cannot read another family's data. Traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. Plan data stays on your phone and is not sent to us.

Children's data

A child account is created and controlled by a parent or guardian. Children do not sign in themselves in this version, and we do not ask for a child's email address, phone number, photo or location. The only child information stored is the name, grade and optional gender the parent enters.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it. When you delete your account, we remove your profile, your memberships, and any family you created (with its children and invitations), unless ownership is transferred to another member of that family.

Your choices

  • Delete your account in the app: Settings → Account → Delete account.
  • Request deletion without the app: use this page.
  • Change your data: edit your profile and children in the app at any time.
